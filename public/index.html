How much of next quarter is actually available for projects?

Most IT teams can't answer that without building a spreadsheet, and the spreadsheet is stale within a fortnight. The harder questions come next: who decided that number, and when did they last look at it?

The difference

BAU is the work that keeps the business running. It belongs in the plan.

Every other tool works out availability from the demand side — it adds up what's been booked and takes that off a notional full week. BAU has nowhere of its own to sit in that model, so it ends up recorded as something it isn't: booked against a placeholder project, logged as leave, or left out altogether. ProjexaR works the other way round. The line manager who owns the team states how much time BAU needs, and project demand is checked against what's left.

How it works

Four things it does, end to end

Projects, workstreams, tasks and sub-tasks with roll-up. Fast task entry — type a name, fill the rest in later. An interactive Gantt with drag-to-reschedule and resize. Predecessor and successor links that cascade when something moves. RAID log, RACI, task notes and RAG status against schedule, budget, scope, resourcing and risk — all in the project, not in five other files.

Line managers set how much BAU each resource carries in each period — as a percentage, days per week or hours per week, whichever way they think. Presets for the common cases, bulk apply across a team, and every new period inherits the last. What's left is project capacity, and ProjexaR works it out the moment the number changes.

Drag a task and ProjexaR checks every assigned resource across the affected range. Where there's a conflict it offers four routes: reassign to someone available, delay until the resource is free, split the work across two people or two windows, or override and flag it. Every decision is the project manager's — the system models options, it doesn't apply them.

Keep a live pipeline of proposed work with a forecast window, a likelihood and a resource model by role type. ProjexaR weighs the demand by how likely each item is, sets it against the capacity you actually have, and shows surplus or shortfall by role type by quarter on a capacity outlook heatmap. Save a dated baseline at a planning cycle and see how the picture has drifted since. Forecasting is part of the paid plan — it's the view that only makes sense once you're running more than two projects at once.
