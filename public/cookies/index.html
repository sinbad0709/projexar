1What this policy covers

This policy explains the storage and access technologies used on projexar.com and on ProjexaR Learning at learning.projexar.com. It covers cookies and also other ways a website can store information in your browser or read it from your device, such as local storage and session storage. UK law treats these technologies alike, so we describe all of them here, not only cookies.

A cookie is a small file that a website asks your browser to keep and that your browser sends back to the website with later requests. Local storage and session storage are areas inside your browser where a page can keep information for its own use. Unlike a cookie, information in them is not sent to the website with each request. Session storage is cleared when you close the tab; local storage stays until it is removed.

How we handle personal data more generally is set out in our Privacy Policy.

6How to control storage

  • ProjexaR Learning progress: switch off “Remember my progress on this device” or reset your progress on the Learning site. Either removes the saved record.
  • Your browser: every major browser lets you view and delete cookies and site data, including local and session storage, for a particular site, and block them in future. Look for “site data” or “cookies and site data” in your browser’s privacy settings.
  • Forms on projexar.com: if you block Cloudflare Turnstile, the forms that use it will not submit. You can email us at privacy@projexar.com instead.
