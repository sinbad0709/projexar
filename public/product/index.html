BAU is set by the person who owns the team.

Line managers set how much BAU — business as usual — each resource carries in each period, as a percentage, days per week or hours per week, whichever way they think. Presets for the common cases, bulk apply across a team, and every new period inherits the last. What's left is project capacity, and ProjexaR works it out the moment the number changes.

Move something and see what it costs before you commit.

Drag a task and ProjexaR checks every assigned resource across the affected range. Where there's a conflict it offers four routes: reassign to someone available, delay until the resource is free, split the work across two people or two windows, or override and flag it. Every decision is the project manager's — the system models options, it doesn't apply them.

Moving Network refresh to week 41 puts Ravi Menon at 118% for three weeks.
Reassign Dee Okafor has 34 days free across the range and holds the same role type.
Delay Start on 2 November and the conflict clears without touching BAU.
Split Across two people, or across two windows either side of the freeze.
Override and flag Proceed at 118%, recorded against your name with a reason.

Answer "can we take this on?" before it's a project.

Keep a live pipeline of proposed work with a forecast window, a likelihood and a resource model by role type. ProjexaR weighs the demand by how likely each item is, sets it against the capacity you actually have, and shows surplus or shortfall by role type by quarter on a capacity outlook heatmap. Save a dated baseline at a planning cycle and see how the picture has drifted since. Forecasting is part of the paid plan — it's the view that only makes sense once you're running more than two projects at once.

The load-bearing point

A project manager cannot overwrite a line manager's BAU.

This is the rule everything else rests on. If a plan doesn't fit, ProjexaR shows the conflict and offers routes around it — it never quietly reduces the time a line manager has reserved for keeping the business running. Every change is written to the log with a timestamp and the name of the person who made it.

  • Set per resource, per period
  • Enforced look-ahead so availability is never blank ahead of the plan
  • Entries can be corrected, never deleted
  • Every affected project manager alerted automatically
