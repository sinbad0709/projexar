1Who we are

This policy explains how ProjexaR (“ProjexaR”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data in connection with the ProjexaR platform and website at projexar.com.

For most of the personal data we hold about our own users and website visitors, we are the controller. For personal data that our customers upload into the platform about their own people and projects, we are the processor and the customer is the controller. Section 6 deals with that relationship separately.

Data protection enquiries: privacy@projexar.com.

2Scope — three groups of people

This policy addresses three distinct groups, whose data we handle differently:

  • Website visitors — people who browse projexar.com (Section 4).
  • Trial and account users — people who sign up for, administer or use a ProjexaR subscription (Section 5).
  • Customer end-users — the resources, staff and contacts whose details a customer uploads into their workspace. We process this data on the customer’s behalf; the customer is the controller (Section 6).

3Regulator and legal framework

We process personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025. References to “the ICO” mean the UK data protection regulator (the office is transitioning to the Information Commission under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025; the regulator continues to operate as the ICO).

4Website visitors

What we collect

Pages visited, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser type, referral source, and any information you submit through a contact or sign-up form. Cookies and similar technologies are covered in our Cookie Policy.

Why, and our lawful basis

  • To operate and secure the website, and to produce aggregate, non-identifying statistics about how it is used — legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) in running and improving our service. First-party, aggregate analytics of this kind operate under the PECR statistical exemption; you can object as described in the Cookie Policy.
  • To respond to enquiries you send us — legitimate interests in answering the people who contact us.
  • Marketing emails, where you have asked for them — consent (Article 6(1)(a)), withdrawable at any time.

Retention

Enquiry correspondence for 6 months; analytics data for 12 months; marketing contacts until you unsubscribe.

5Trial and account users

What we collect

Your name, work email, hashed password, the organisation you represent, subscription and billing status, records of your use of the product, and support correspondence. Card details are handled entirely by our payment processor and never reach ProjexaR systems (Section 8).

Why, and our lawful basis

Purposes for which we process account data, and the lawful basis for each.
Purpose Lawful basis
Creating and running your account; providing the platform Performance of a contract (Art 6(1)(b))
Taking payment and managing the subscription and the 90-day guarantee Performance of a contract (Art 6(1)(b))
Keeping financial and tax records Legal obligation (Art 6(1)(c))
Securing the platform, preventing misuse, and product improvement Legitimate interests (Art 6(1)(f))
Service and security notifications Legitimate interests (Art 6(1)(f))
Optional marketing about features and offers Consent (Art 6(1)(a)), withdrawable

We rely on legitimate interests only where our interest is not overridden by your rights; a summary balancing assessment is available on request.

Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you by solely automated means. The AI-assisted replanning within the product suggests schedules for a human to accept or reject; it does not decide anything about a data subject.

Retention

Account data for the life of the subscription plus 3 months; billing records for 12 months to meet statutory requirements; unconverted trial data as described in the Data Export and Deletion Policy.

6Customer end-user data (we are the processor)

When a customer uploads data about their resources and projects — names, work email addresses, job roles, cost or charge rates, allocations, project names, BAU days per week, and availability or absence (recorded without any reason for absence) — the customer is the controller and we process that data only on their documented instructions, under our Data Processing Agreement.

We do not record reasons for absence, sickness or leave, and the platform is not designed to hold special category data (Article 9) or criminal offence data. Customers are instructed not to input such data.

If you are a customer end-user and wish to exercise your rights, please contact the customer organisation that holds your data; we will assist them as our DPA requires. Where the law requires us to act, or where you contact us directly, we will pass your request to the relevant customer and support its handling.

Sub-processors

We use the third parties listed in our Sub-processor Register to deliver the service. The AI-assisted replanning uses one or more third-party AI models. Where a model is self-hosted within our own infrastructure, your data is processed on our systems and is not shared with the model’s provider. Where a model is accessed as a hosted service, the provider is named in our Sub-processor Register together with the data sent, its retention, and our contractual position on training. Full detail is in our AI Processing Statement.

7International transfers

Each customer’s platform data is held in a single cloud region. UK and EEA customer data is held in the UK/EEA. Where any transfer of personal data outside the UK is required — for example to a controller-side vendor operating overseas — we rely on UK adequacy regulations where they apply, or on the ICO’s International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, supported by a transfer risk assessment applying the “data protection test” under the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025. Details are in our International Transfer Addendum and Sub-processor Register.

8Payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details are collected and processed by them under their own terms and never pass through or rest on ProjexaR systems. We receive only confirmation of payment and limited billing metadata.

9Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the UK GDPR, you have the right to access your data; to rectification; to erasure; to restriction; to object; to data portability; and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. You also have rights in relation to automated decision-making where it applies.

To exercise a right, contact privacy@projexar.com. We respond within one month. We may pause that period where we reasonably need you to confirm your identity or clarify your request, resuming once you respond; and we will carry out reasonable and proportionate searches to locate your data (Data Protection Act 2018 as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025). There is normally no charge.

10Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first at privacy@projexar.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay. This reflects the controller complaints duty in force from 19 June 2026.

If we cannot resolve it, you may complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or by post to Information Commissioner’s Office, Wycombe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF. The ICO may ask you to raise the matter with us first.

11Security

We protect personal data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and the measures described on our Trust page and in our Security Questionnaire Pre-Answer Pack. No system is perfectly secure; our Personal Data Breach Procedure governs how we respond to any incident.

12Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified to account holders and reflected in the control block above. The current version always governs.
