What "two projects" actually means

Two projects running at the same time. When a project finishes, archive it — it stays available to read, with its full history and audit trail intact, and it no longer counts toward your two. Start the next one whenever you're ready. Nothing is deleted, and nothing needs deleting to stay within the free plan.

You licence a number. That number is your bill.

Licence the highest number of resources you expect to run. No daily counting, no usage statement, no reconciliation at the end of the month — the figure is set when you buy it, and it's the same every cycle.

What counts

A managed resource is a person who can be assigned to project work. If someone can appear on a plan, they count. If they only need to view plans, approve requests or run reports, they don't — logins are unlimited and free. On the free plan nobody is counted at all.

Monthly — change it whenever

Your licence count is yours to adjust at any time. When you reach it, ProjexaR will ask you to add licences before assigning anyone else; you do that yourself and carry on.

Annual — fixed, with room to move

Your count is set for the year, and it comes with room for two more resources at no charge. Go beyond that and nothing is blocked — you have 30 days to come back down. If you're still above your licensed count on day 30, we adjust your licence to match the number in use that day. Not the highest number we saw.

Ninety days to change your mind

If ProjexaR isn't right for you, tell us within 90 days of your first payment and we'll refund it. Your data is yours throughout — export it whenever you want, including on the way out.

Questions people ask before signing up

Is the free plan really free forever?
Yes. Two active projects, as many people as you want, no card, no expiry. If two projects is all you ever run, you'll never pay us anything.
What happens when a project finishes?
Archive it. It stays readable with its full history, and it stops counting toward your two. Start the next project straight away.
What's different on the paid plan?
The number of projects you can run at once, and the forecasting that only makes sense across a portfolio — the capacity outlook, pipeline modelling and forecast baselines. Everything else is in both.
Do you charge for people we only plan around?
On the paid plan, yes, and only for those people. Anyone assignable to project work counts as a managed resource. Anyone who just needs access — managers, approvers, viewers — logs in free.
Can we import what we've already got?
Yes, on the free plan as well. ProjexaR reads MS Project files directly, along with Excel and exported Planner plans. People, projects, assignments and allocation percentages all come across.
Is there a minimum term?
No. Monthly is monthly. Annual is paid upfront for the discount and carries the same 90-day guarantee.
Can we change the number of resources?
On monthly, at any time — add licences yourself and they're available immediately. On annual, your count is fixed for the year, with room for two more resources included and a 30-day window if you go beyond it.
Can we go back to the free plan?
Yes. Come back down to two active projects and archive the rest — your data stays where it is.
Do you count our resources every day?
No. Your licence is set when you buy it and that's what you're billed. There's no daily snapshot, no usage statement and nothing to reconcile. On annual plans there's a single check: if you've been above your licensed count for 30 days, we adjust it to match.
What if we need more people part-way through an annual term?
Two extra resources run at no charge and nothing is blocked. Beyond that, a 30-day window opens. Come back down inside it and nothing changes. Still above on day 30, and your licence moves to the count in use that day — never the peak. Short-term spikes for a migration or a cutover cost you nothing.
